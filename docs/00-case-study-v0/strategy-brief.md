# Strategy Brief (condensed)

## Problem
Travel agencies sell "the perfect trip" but deliver a rushed, one-size-fits
-all itinerary: fixed hours per stop, commission-driven restaurants and
souvenir stops. The alternative — full DIY planning — is a second job.

## Value proposition
"Be part of planning your trip, without doing all the research yourself."
A structured, opinionated planning process (refined since 2017) instead of
an agency's rigid package or a blank spreadsheet.

## Target users
- Solo Explorer: travels alone, wants curated options without weeks of
  research.
- Designated Planner: always ends up planning the group trip, needs a way
  to turn many opinions into one itinerary without endless group-chat
  back-and-forth.

## Differentiation
Competitors are point solutions (itinerary mapping OR bookings OR group
voting OR AI suggestions). Andanzas' edge is a field-tested curation
methodology plus solo+group in one system — not generic AI-generated
itineraries.

## Roadmap
- Now: curated place options + group/solo voting (this sprint)
- Next: finance/budget tracking, reservations & tickets vault
- Later: PWA/offline, native mobile, monetization model
