# Personas & Jobs to Be Done

## Persona A — The Solo Explorer, Not a Loner
25–38, travels alone 1-2x/year internationally.
- Functional job: "Help me build a trip that fits how I actually like to
  travel, without spending every evening for a month researching it."
- Emotional job: "Let me feel like I discovered this place myself."
- Social job: "Give me something worth telling friends about."

## Persona B — The Designated Planner
30–50, plans trips for 4-10 people (friends, siblings, multi-gen family).
- Functional job: "Help me turn ten opinions into one itinerary everyone
  agrees to, without forty back-and-forth messages."
- Emotional job: "Take the pressure off me — win or lose, don't make me
  the only reason."
- Social job: "Let me look like I have this handled, effortlessly."
